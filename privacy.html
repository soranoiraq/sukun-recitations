Privacy Policy — Sukun Recitations Publisher

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This application ("Sukun Recitations Publisher") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Sukun Recitations | سُكون التلاوة" to upload and schedule the channel's own Quran recitation videos through the YouTube Data API.

What data it accesses

  • The owner's YouTube channel information (channel name and ID), to confirm uploads go to the correct channel.
  • Permission to upload videos to that channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored locally on the owner's computer (macOS Keychain) and is never shared, sold or sent to any third party.
  • The application does not collect data about viewers or any other users.

Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Contact

For any question, contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@sukunrecitationsquran
